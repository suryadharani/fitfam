GENTLE FEEDBACK

Supportive, non-judgmental insights

FitFam calculates helpful mathematical milestones from user-entered logs without clinical judgment or stressful labels.

📉 Week-over-Week

“↓ 0.3 kg from last week”

Calculated directly from your previous week’s log to show short-term changes.

🎯 Baseline Progress

“↓ 0.9 kg since your first entry”

Shows how far you have come from your initial check-in date across all recorded weeks.

📅 Consistency Badge

“5 weeks recorded”

Celebrates your habit of showing up and checking in on your chosen weekly day.

🌊 Trend Analysis

“Recent trend: Gradually decreasing”

Smooths out natural daily water weight fluctuations to reveal your overarching trajectory.

📊 Rolling Average

“4-week average: 78.8 kg”

Eliminates temporary spikes so you get a realistic view of your true monthly baseline.

🌿 Non-Clinical

Purely factual & private

We never label anyone as “healthy” or “unhealthy”. Your data belongs only to you.
