# 🌿 FitFam — Your family's weekly weigh-in, made simple.

> "Record it. Track it. Understand it."

FitFam is a private, invite-only family weekly weight tracking application. Family members can record their weight once a week and FitFam turns their accumulated history into simple, meaningful personal insights and trends.

## 🎯 Core Features & Philosophy

* **Stress-Free Weekly Cadence:** No daily logging or calorie counting. Check in once a week.
* **Universal Family Setup:** Add any family member, roommate, or relative with custom names and individual reminder schedules.
* **Insightful Trends:** Calculates rolling 4-week averages, week-over-week deltas, and trajectory milestones purely from user history.
* **Non-Clinical & Gentle:** Designed to be supportive and non-judgmental without medical labels or diagnoses.
* **100% Private & Invite-Only:** Access is curated to maintain family intimacy. Request invitations via [@travelwithnani](https://ig.me/m/travelwithnani).
