Account & Data Deletion Portal

📱 Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the FitFam application installed on your Android device or Web browser, you can immediately delete your account and all household records yourself:

  1. Open FitFam and navigate to Settings.
  2. Select the Password & Security tab.
  3. Scroll to Delete FitFam Account.
  4. Re-authenticate with your password or Google account and confirm deletion.

🌐 Option 2: Web Account Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or no longer have access to your device, you can submit an account deletion request below. We will verify ownership of the registered account email address and permanently delete your account within 48 hours.

⚠️ What gets deleted:
  • Your user account profile and authentication record
  • All created family member profiles in your household
  • All historical weigh-in logs, milestone statistics, and trend entries

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
